CWan Lab @ UChicago

At CWan Lab (pronounced “Wan Lab”), we advance the foundations of artificial intelligence and machine learning to address key challenges in health, with a focus on spatially resolved biomedical data, multimodal clinical data, and interpretable models.

Our work bridges algorithmic innovation and real-world application. We develop biologically interpretable and clinically impactful models that uncover disease mechanisms and drive personalized medicine.

We are part of the Institute for Population and Precision Health at the University of Chicago, with work in venues including AAAI, IJCAI, MICCAI, ICDM, ML4H, The Lancet Oncology, and Briefings in Bioinformatics.

{% include carousel.html %}
We are recruiting: postdoctoral researchers and research assistants with expertise in AI, large language models, and spatial biology, particularly as applied to cancer and precision health. UChicago students are welcome too. See our openings page.

Research Directions

We build learning methods for biomedical data and carry them through to clinical questions in oncology and dermatology.
